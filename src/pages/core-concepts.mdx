## 1. Reactive vs. Proactive

**Reactive** systems wait. They fire a passive notification and leave everything to the user. If the user swipes it away, the system has failed.

**Proactive** systems take the initiative. LAFINA starts a high-priority interaction when something is due: a full-screen call that the student answers and responds to.

<Card title="Simulated Voice Calls">
  LAFINA shows a ringing, full-screen incoming call, even over the lock screen. People are conditioned to answer calls far more than they read notifications.
</Card>

<Card title="Active Acknowledgment">
  The student responds by voice ("okay", "give me ten minutes") or with a button. This makes them actually process the reminder instead of swiping it away.
</Card>

<Card title="Closed Loop">
  LAFINA handles the whole exchange: it rings, speaks, listens, acknowledges or snoozes, and ends the call. Declined or missed calls snooze automatically, up to a limit.
</Card>

## 2. The AI Student Companion

LAFINA is more than a scheduler. A companion is something students *want* to open, not only something that nags them. LAFINA:

* chats naturally and answers general and trivia questions (Online Mode),
* explains lessons and helps with study tools (flashcards, quizzes, Pomodoro, summaries),
* remembers preferences like study hours or a preferred name,
* gives a **Morning Briefing** of the day after the alarm,
* answers USTP questions with sources.

When students already talk to LAFINA every day, its reminders and planning help become part of their routine.

## 3. Offline-First

The phone's SQLite database is the source of truth. Planning, alarms, reminder calls, and supported voice commands work with no internet. Online services add to the core but never replace it, and if they fail, LAFINA falls back to on-device replies.

## 4. The AI Proposes, the Student Decides

Any change the assistant wants to make (add, edit, or delete) appears as a **review card**. The student can edit, cancel, or confirm. This keeps AI mistakes from turning into wrong reminders and keeps the student in control. Only small, easily undone actions (completing a task, snoozing, acknowledging, pinning a note) apply immediately, with an Undo option.

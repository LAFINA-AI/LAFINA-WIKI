## Alarm vs. reminder

| | Alarm | Reminder |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Wake up at a time | Remember a deadline, class, or activity |
| **Alert** | Native ringing screen with Dismiss / Snooze | Simulated incoming call, or notification only |
| **Speech** | Optional **Morning Briefing** after dismiss | Spoken reminder + voice reply |
| **Sync** | ❌ Stays on this phone | ✅ Synced with an account |
| **Table** | `alarms` | `reminders` |

## How a reminder gets scheduled

<FlowChart
  label="Scheduling a reminder"
  steps={[
    { title: 'Student creates the reminder', text: 'Manually, or by confirming a review card', kind: 'start' },
    { title: 'Saved to SQLite', text: 'reminders row (status = pending) + sync outbox, one transaction', kind: 'store' },
    { title: 'Registered with Android AlarmManager', text: 'With owner + generation number', kind: 'end' },
  ]}
/>

- **Lead time:** a reminder can ring before the item it's about, with lead times of up to **24 hours**.
- **Delivery style per reminder:** **Call me** (simulated call) or **Notify only** (a notification with Acknowledge and Snooze buttons).
- **Generation number:** every rescheduling increases a generation. A late or old alarm with an outdated generation is ignored, so a stale alarm can never ring for a reminder that has moved.
- SQLite and Android scheduling are not one atomic transaction, so a **reconciler** re-registers alarms from the database after changes.

<Alert type="warning" title="Acknowledged ≠ task done">
  Acknowledging only means "I heard it". The linked task stays pending until the student completes it. If the task is <em>already</em> completed when the alarm fires, the call doesn't ring.
</Alert>

**Fallbacks:** if the microphone or Whisper is unavailable, the call shows **Acknowledge / Snooze buttons**. Buttons are always available.

**Online version:** if the student is online with online voice enabled, LAFINA first speaks the reminder and then hands the call to a **live online conversation** (Lesson 7). If the internet drops mid-call, it switches back to on-device voice.

**Time limits:** an answered call is limited to about 10 minutes, and a queued call to about 15 minutes.

## Snooze rules

| Setting | How it works |
|---|---|
| Spoken snooze | The minutes you say ("give me 10 minutes" → 10) |
| Default / auto-snooze length | From the student's preferences (5 minutes by default) |
| Snooze limit | When the limit is reached, an ignored call marks the reminder **missed** instead of snoozing again |

## Reliability: why reminders still ring

<Card title="What LAFINA does">
  <ul>
    <li>Uses Android <strong>AlarmManager</strong> exact alarms, not an in-app timer.</li>
    <li><strong>Recovery receivers</strong> re-register pending alarms after a <strong>reboot</strong> or a <strong>timezone change</strong>.</li>
    <li>Records <strong>delivery jobs and events</strong> (<code>reminder_delivery_jobs</code>, <code>reminder_delivery_events</code>) for recovery and audit.</li>
    <li>Adopts snoozes that native code applied while the app's JavaScript wasn't running.</li>
    <li>Provides <strong>Profile → Alarm & Reminder Health</strong>: permission checks plus a test alarm.</li>
  </ul>
</Card>

**Honest limitation:** notification, exact-alarm, and full-screen permissions, plus aggressive battery saving on some brands (some Infinix, Tecno, and itel phones), can delay alerts. The fix is to set LAFINA's battery setting to *Unrestricted*.

## Morning Briefing

After dismissing an alarm, LAFINA can speak a short **briefing** of the day (classes, tasks, reminders), personalized with Memory. It runs locally with the phone's voice.

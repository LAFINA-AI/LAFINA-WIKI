## Background

Existing tools like standard alarms and push notifications are easily ignored. They lack the urgency and personal connection needed to enforce task compliance. 

<Alert type="warning" title="The Problem with Current AI">
  While AI assistants exist (e.g., Siri, Alexa), they are primarily reactive and heavily require cloud connectivity, making them less accessible in areas with poor internet or for users conscious about privacy.
</Alert>

## Core Objectives

Our project aims to solve this by achieving the following specific goals:

1. **Voice-First Reminder App**: Develop a mobile app that uses simulated human-like phone calls to deliver reminders.
2. **On-Device NLP**: Implement natural language processing directly on the device to convert voice interactions into structured schedule data.
3. **Usability Testing**: Ensure the system is user-friendly, non-intrusive, and effective at improving schedule adherence.

<Card title="Why Voice-First?">
  A simulated phone call demands immediate attention and cognitive engagement, drastically reducing the likelihood of a reminder being passively swiped away.
</Card>

## Initial Setup (Use Case)

The user taps the microphone input. The AI receives the input, crafts a response, creates a schedule specifying the delivery time, and saves it to the local SQLite database job queue.

## The Proactive Call Flow

The core innovation of LAFINA is its proactive call delivery system, which operates in six distinct stages:

<FlowDiagram />

## Stage Breakdown

1. **Trigger** — The background SQLite job queue detects that a specific reminder time has been reached and wakes the call service.

2. **Call Initiation** — Instead of a push notification, the application triggers an actual outgoing phone call to the user's device using native Android telephony interfaces.

3. **TTS Delivery** — When the user answers, the Kokoro 82M TTS module verbally delivers the pre-crafted reminder message over the active audio line.

4. **STT Acknowledgment** — The user's verbal response is captured over the live call, processed by Whisper.cpp, and converted to a text transcript in real time.

5. **Intent Evaluation** — SmolLM2 analyzes the transcript to determine intent. It understands natural phrasing: *"Give me five more minutes"* triggers a re-queue, not a failure.

6. **Termination** — Upon confirmed acknowledgment, the AI generates a brief closing statement, automatically ends the call, and marks the job queue entry as complete.

## Why Not a Simple Alarm?

A standard alarm or push notification is a **zero-turn** interaction — fire and forget. LAFINA's call flow is **multi-turn and bi-directional**: the AI initiates, the user responds, the AI evaluates, and the AI closes the loop. This is what separates a voice-call reminder system from a glorified notification.

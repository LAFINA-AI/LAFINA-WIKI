<Card title="The four ideas everything else builds on">
  <ol>
    <li><strong>Offline-first:</strong> the phone's SQLite database is the source of truth. Online is optional.</li>
    <li><strong>The AI proposes, the student confirms:</strong> planner changes go through a review card.</li>
    <li><strong>Proactive calls:</strong> reminders ring as a simulated call that the student answers and responds to.</li>
    <li><strong>A companion:</strong> with Online Mode, LAFINA chats about anything, answers trivia, and helps with studying.</li>
  </ol>
</Card>

Despite this scale, people mostly use voice assistants for calls, music, and searches. Only **4.3%** use them for alarms or reminders, the most practical scheduling use case.

## The Localized Problem (Philippines Context)

The Philippines has high mobile penetration (over 90%), and roughly 57% of the population owns a smartphone. Students carry heavy loads: subjects, projects, organizations, and often part-time work. Their plans end up scattered across calendars, chat threads, notes apps, and memory.

Two problems follow:

1. **Fragmentation.** Schedules, tasks, notes, and school information sit in different places, so students forget or double-book.
2. **Alert fatigue.** Constant push notifications teach users to ignore them. Healthcare studies show that telephone call reminders can reduce missed appointments by up to:

<div style="display:flex; gap:1rem; flex-wrap:wrap; margin:1rem 0 1.5rem;">
  <StatCard value="39%" label="reduction in missed appointments with call reminders vs. push notifications" source="Mehranbod et al." color="warning" />
</div>

LAFINA addresses both: one companion app that holds a student's academic life and delivers important reminders as a call.

## General Objective

To design, develop, and evaluate **LAFINA**, an AI student companion for Android that helps students at the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP), Cagayan de Oro, and beyond organize their academic life. LAFINA delivers proactive call-style reminders and keeps core planning available without internet.

## Specific Objectives

1. **Provide** manual, text, and voice ways to manage tasks, events, time blocks, reminders, alarms, and notes.
2. **Deliver** reminders as simulated phone calls that the student acknowledges or snoozes by voice or button, using Android alarms that work even when the app is closed.
3. **Implement** on-device speech recognition and intent understanding so that supported commands work offline.
4. **Offer** a conversational companion: open conversation, general questions, and study help with Online Mode, and small talk and planner help offline.
5. **Ensure** user control: the assistant proposes planner changes that the student reviews and confirms before they are saved.
6. **Support** USTP students with COR schedule import and **Ask USTP** answers that show their sources.
7. **Synchronize** personal records through an optional account while keeping the phone as the working copy.
8. **Evaluate** usability (System Usability Scale), correctness, and reliability on real devices.

## Scope and Limitations

<Card title="In Scope">
  * Personal academic planning: tasks, events, time blocks, reminders, notes, alarms.
  * Conversation: small talk, general and trivia questions, explanations, and study help (open conversation requires Online Mode).
  * Study skills: Pomodoro, flashcards, quizzes, GWA calculator, summarizer, lecture recording and transcription, document generation.
  * USTP features: COR import and Ask USTP (handbook and campus information with sources).
  * Guest mode on the phone, plus optional account sync.
</Card>

<Card title="Out of Scope / Limitations">
  * Android only (Android 11 or later). No iOS.
  * No login to or integration with university portals or learning management systems. The COR is a file the student provides.
  * No Gmail or inbox integration, and no control of other apps.
  * No wake word and no always-on listening.
  * Open-ended conversation and AI generation need internet. Offline understanding covers supported commands and small talk.
  * Alarm timing can be affected by aggressive battery saving on some phone brands.
  * LAFINA is a personal assistant. It does not replace official university advice.
</Card>

## Setting a Reminder

A student creates a reminder by tapping, typing, or speaking, for example *"Remind me to submit my lab report tomorrow at 5 PM."* LAFINA understands the request, shows a **review card** with the title, date, and time, and saves it to the phone's SQLite database once the student confirms. The reminder is then registered with Android's alarm system.

## Stage Breakdown

1. **Trigger.** Android's `AlarmManager` fires at the reminder time, even if LAFINA is closed. LAFINA first checks that the reminder is still valid: right account, still pending, and task not already completed.

2. **Incoming call.** A full-screen simulated call appears, even over the lock screen. It is shown inside LAFINA and is not a real telephone call, so it needs no SIM and works offline.

3. **Spoken reminder.** When the student answers, LAFINA reads the reminder aloud with the phone's text-to-speech. If online voice is enabled and available, the call can continue into a live online conversation.

4. **Listen.** The microphone opens and **Whisper.cpp** transcribes the reply on the phone.

5. **Understand the reply.** A keyword matcher understands natural replies: *"okay"*, *"got it"* → acknowledge; *"give me five more minutes"* → snooze for 5 minutes. If the reply isn't understood, LAFINA asks again, and after 3 tries it snoozes automatically.

6. **Close the loop.** LAFINA confirms the outcome aloud, ends the call, and saves the result. If the student declines or doesn't answer, the reminder auto-snoozes. Once the snooze limit is reached, it is marked **missed**.

<Alert type="warning" title="Acknowledged ≠ Done">
  Acknowledging a reminder does not complete its task. The task stays pending until the student marks it done, so a half-awake "okay" doesn't hide unfinished work.
</Alert>

## Why Not a Simple Alarm?

A standard alarm or push notification is a **zero-turn** interaction: it fires and is forgotten. LAFINA's call is **multi-turn and two-way**: LAFINA starts the call, the student responds, LAFINA interprets the reply, and LAFINA closes the loop.

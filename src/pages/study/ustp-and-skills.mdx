## What is RAG?

**Retrieval-Augmented Generation:** first **retrieve** relevant source passages, then **generate** an answer *from those passages*, and show the sources. This reduces made-up answers because the reply must be supported by evidence.

## Ask USTP sources

- The **USTP Student Handbook (2023 edition)**.
- The **USTP Cagayan de Oro knowledge snapshot**: AY 2026–2027 semester, exam and enrollment dates, offices and contacts, PRISMS and other portals, the library, programs, scholarships, and student organizations.

USTP features follow the student's **USTP eligibility** (student setup). A handbook chat never reaches retrieval without it.

## Study skills

| Skill | What it does | Needs internet? |
|---|---|---|
| Pomodoro / Focus timer | Timed focus sessions, started from Home gaps too | No |
| Flashcards | Study decks with review | No to review. Yes to AI-generate |
| Quiz maker | Quizzes from your material | Yes (AI generation) |
| GWA calculator | Semester general weighted average | No |
| Summarizer | Summaries of uploaded material | Yes |
| Lesson Q&A | Ask questions about your uploaded study material (local library + chunked search) | Yes for AI answers |
| Recording / transcription | Record lectures. Transcribed locally with Whisper | No to transcribe. Yes for AI notes |
| Documents | Generate PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint | Yes |
| To-dos, notes, alarms | Core tools | No |

**Privacy detail:** for AI meeting notes, only the **transcript text** is sent, after consent. The recording audio is not uploaded.

LAFINA relies on a robust 3-layer architecture designed for entirely offline execution.

### 1. UI/UX Layer
This layer is built using **React Native**, serving as the primary mobile app interface. It handles background job queues, schedule management screens, and the critical microphone input required for voice interaction.

### 2. AI Layer
The core intelligence of LAFINA runs completely offline on the device using GGML/C++ binaries.

<Card title="AI Models Used">
- **Whisper.cpp (Tiny/Base quantized)**: Provides accurate and fast on-device Speech-to-Text capabilities.
- **SmolLM2-135M-Instruct**: Responsible for intent parsing and crafting the natural language responses based on the student's context.
- **Kokoro 82M**: Handles Text-to-Speech synthesis to deliver human-like audio.
</Card>

### 3. Storage Layer
Local persistence is achieved using **SQLite** and **Async Storage**. This layer securely keeps track of student schedules, pre-crafted AI responses, and trigger times without ever sending data to the cloud.

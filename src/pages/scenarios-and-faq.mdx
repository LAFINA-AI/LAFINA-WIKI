## Scenarios

For the full side-by-side paradigm comparison, visit the [Proactive vs. Reactive](/proactive-vs-reactive) page.

### Overcoming Notification Fatigue
A student has their phone on silent mode during a busy day. They completely miss a standard, silent push notification for an upcoming assignment. However, because LAFINA triggers an audible, native phone call, the student's attention is immediately captured — silent mode does not suppress the native call ringer.

### Intent-Based Acknowledgment
LAFINA calls a student to remind them of a quiz in ten minutes. The student is currently finishing up a meal and replies, "Give me five more minutes." The AI comprehends this complex intent and proactively adjusts the schedule to call back slightly later.

### Autonomous Execution (Offline)
A student is commuting home through an area with no cell service or Wi-Fi. Because LAFINA's AI models run entirely on the local device hardware, the student still receives their critical study reminder call precisely on time.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<Card title="Is LAFINA a chatbot?">
  No, LAFINA is a closed-domain, task-oriented spoken dialogue system (TOSDS). It does not engage in general chit-chat or answer trivia questions. Its sole purpose is to manage and enforce your academic schedule. See the [Glossary](/glossary) for a definition of TOSDS.
</Card>

<Card title="Does it need Wi-Fi or data to work?">
  No. LAFINA is completely offline-first. All Voice-to-Text (Whisper.cpp), Language Modeling (SmolLM2), and Text-to-Speech (Kokoro) processing occur directly on your phone's processor via GGML binaries.
</Card>

<Card title="Why does it call me instead of sending a text?">
  Text messages and push notifications contribute to "alert fatigue" and are easily ignored. A simulated phone call forces you to actively answer and verbally engage, vastly improving task compliance and awareness. Healthcare studies show this approach reduces missed appointments by up to 39%.
</Card>

<Card title="Is my voice data sent to the cloud?">
  No. Because the entire AI layer operates via GGML binaries directly on your device, your voice data and schedule details are never transmitted over the internet, ensuring complete privacy.
</Card>

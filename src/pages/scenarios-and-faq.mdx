## Scenarios

For the full paradigm comparison, visit the [Proactive vs. Reactive](/proactive-vs-reactive) page.

### Overcoming Notification Fatigue
A student's phone is locked in their bag. A normal notification for an assignment deadline goes unnoticed. LAFINA rings with a full-screen call over the lock screen, reads the reminder aloud, and waits for a response.

### Natural Replies During a Call
LAFINA calls about a quiz in ten minutes. The student says, *"Give me five more minutes."* LAFINA snoozes the reminder for five minutes and calls back.

### Planning by Voice
A student says, *"Block two hours for my thesis on Saturday morning."* LAFINA shows a review card with the time block and warns about any overlap with existing classes. One tap on Confirm saves it.

### A Study Companion
At night, a student asks, *"Can you explain photosynthesis simply?"* and then, *"Make flashcards from my biology notes."* With Online Mode, LAFINA explains the topic and opens the flashcard skill.

### Just Talking
After a long day, a student types *"I'm so tired, how are you?"* LAFINA replies warmly. If the student mentions *"I'm a night owl"*, LAFINA can save it to Memory and use it later when suggesting study times.

### Offline Commute
With no signal, the student's reminder still rings on time, and voice commands like *"What's on my schedule tomorrow?"* still work, because they run on the phone.

### USTP Questions
A student asks, *"When is the last day of enrollment?"* or *"What's the retention policy?"* **Ask USTP** answers from the handbook and campus knowledge pack and shows the source.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<Card title="Is LAFINA a chatbot?">
  It's an <strong>AI student companion</strong>. With Online Mode, it chats about anything, answers general and trivia questions, and helps you study. At its core, it's also a planner that manages your schedule and calls you for reminders. Any change to your planner still needs your confirmation.
</Card>

<Card title="Does it need Wi-Fi or data to work?">
  Not for the core. Tasks, notes, the calendar, alarms, reminder calls, small talk, and supported voice commands work offline. Open conversation, trivia, AI study generation, online voice, and sync need internet.
</Card>

<Card title="Why does it call me instead of sending a notification?">
  Notifications are easy to swipe away (alert fatigue). A call asks you to answer and respond, so you actually notice the reminder. Healthcare studies show call reminders cut missed appointments by up to 39%.
</Card>

<Card title="Is it a real phone call?">
  No. It's a simulated call screen inside LAFINA. It doesn't use your SIM, carrier, or load.
</Card>

<Card title="Is my voice data sent to the cloud?">
  Push-to-talk, reminder calls, and the offline Call LAFINA transcribe speech on your phone. Only the <strong>online</strong> Call LAFINA streams audio to ElevenLabs, and only during a call you started, after you give consent.
</Card>

<Card title="Can the AI change my schedule by itself?">
  No. The assistant prepares a review card, and nothing is saved until you confirm. Only small, easily undone actions (like snoozing or completing a task) apply right away, with Undo.
</Card>

## Scope & Limitations

<Card title="⚠️ Some Features Need Online Mode">
  Offline, LAFINA handles small talk and planning commands but will ask you to turn on Online Mode for general-knowledge questions, trivia, and AI-generated study material.
</Card>

<Card title="⚠️ Acoustic Constraint: Background Noise">
  On-device speech recognition (Whisper <code>tiny.en</code>) works best in reasonably quiet places. Loud cafeterias or streets can cause misheard replies, and LAFINA may ask you to repeat yourself. Buttons and typing are always available.
</Card>

<Card title="⚠️ Phone Battery Policies">
  Some phone brands delay alarms when the screen is off to save battery. Use <strong>Profile → Alarm & Reminder Health</strong> and set LAFINA's battery setting to <em>Unrestricted</em> for reliable reminders.
</Card>

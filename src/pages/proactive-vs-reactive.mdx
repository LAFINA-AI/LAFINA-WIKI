## Two Paradigms, One Problem

Most reminder tools are **reactive**. The user sets an alarm or reminder, and the system passively fires a notification. If the user ignores it, the system has failed.

LAFINA is **proactive**: when something is due, it rings with a call, speaks the reminder, and waits for the student's response before closing the loop.

## Why This Matters: Alert Fatigue

The average smartphone user receives **65–80 push notifications per day** (Localytics, 2023). At that volume, the brain starts treating notifications as background noise. This is called **alert fatigue**.

Healthcare research shows the difference: patients who received appointment reminder texts had a no-show rate of about 27%, while those who received telephone call reminders had a rate of only about 16%, a **39% relative reduction** (Mehranbod et al.). LAFINA applies the same idea to academic schedules.

## The Three Proactive Mechanisms

<Card title="1. Simulated Voice Calls">
  Instead of a silent banner, LAFINA shows a ringing, full-screen incoming call, even over the lock screen. People are conditioned to pick up calls.
</Card>

<Card title="2. Active Engagement">
  A notification can be dismissed without thinking. A call asks the student to answer, listen, and respond, so they actually process the reminder.
</Card>

<Card title="3. Closed-Loop Confirmation">
  The reminder isn't settled until the student acknowledges or snoozes it. Replies like "give me five more minutes" are understood and reschedule the call. Ignored calls auto-snooze and ring again, up to a limit.
</Card>

## Real-World Scenarios

### Scenario A: The Locked Phone

A student's phone is locked in their bag during a study session. A normal notification just lights the screen for a moment. LAFINA's reminder call appears **over the lock screen** with Answer and Decline buttons, and Decline snoozes it without unlocking.

### Scenario B: Natural Replies

LAFINA calls ten minutes before a group meeting. The student is finishing a meal and says *"Give me five more minutes."* A normal alarm only knows Dismiss and Snooze. LAFINA understands the reply as a **five-minute snooze** and rings again then.

### Scenario C: No Signal

A student commutes through an area with no signal. The reminder still rings on time, because the alarm, the call screen, speech recognition (Whisper), and the phone's voice all run **on the device**.

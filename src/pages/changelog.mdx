## Feature Milestones

### Offline Voice Pipeline
<Badge variant="success">Oct 25–26</Badge>

Push-to-talk speech recognition with whisper.cpp and Silero VAD through a native module, plus fast spoken replies through the phone's own TTS engine. The earlier Kokoro TTS plan was dropped in favor of the built-in engine, which starts faster and uses less memory.

### Hybrid Understanding
<Badge variant="success">Oct 27 – Nov 16</Badge>

A rule-based fast-path parser for clear commands, with an on-device language model (via llama.cpp) for unclear or Taglish phrasing. This later grew into a selectable catalog (SmolLM2, Gemma 3, Qwen 2.5) with a hardware advisor.

### Confirm-Before-Save
<Badge variant="success">Nov 6</Badge>

Every assistant-proposed change became a **review card** with revisions and receipts. Low-risk, easily undone actions were later allowed to apply immediately with Undo.

### From Scheduler to Companion
<Badge variant="success">Nov 9–22</Badge>

DeepSeek-powered conversation, Memory, the Chat agent, ElevenLabs live voice, study skills, and Lesson Q&A turned LAFINA into an **AI student companion** that can chat, answer general questions, and help with studying.

### Proactive Reminder Calls
<Badge variant="success">Oct 23 → Nov 25</Badge>

Native Android alarms with recovery after reboot came first. Later additions: per-reminder "Call me / Notify only", online reminder calls that continue into live conversation, faster call responses, and lock-screen calls (v1.0.7).

## Key Design Decisions

| Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|
| Android only (no iOS) | Full-screen call screens, exact alarms, and background audio rely on Android features |
| SQLite as source of truth | Offline-first: planning and reminders never depend on a server |
| Rules before models | Instant, predictable, and light on battery. Models only where needed |
| Phone TTS instead of Kokoro | Already installed, faster first audio, lower memory |
| Simulated call instead of real telephony | No SIM or carrier needed, works offline, full control of the call UI |
| Online AI is optional | Adds open conversation and natural voice without making the core depend on internet |
| Confirm-before-save | Keeps the student in control and turns AI mistakes into corrections |

## Two Kinds of Dialogue in One Assistant

### 1. Task-Oriented Dialogue (the planner core) <Badge variant="success">Offline</Badge>

For planning, LAFINA behaves like a **Task-Oriented Spoken Dialogue System (TOSDS)**. It understands a specific set of intents (create, update, or delete tasks, reminders, notes, time blocks, and alarms; query schedule; snooze; acknowledge) and fills slots such as title, date, and time. This part works offline with the rule parser and on-device models.

### 2. Open-Domain Conversation (the companion) <Badge variant="info">Online Mode</Badge>

With Online Mode, LAFINA can talk about almost anything a student brings up: small talk, trivia, explaining a lesson, study tips, or just chatting after a long day. This is powered by DeepSeek. LAFINA also remembers personal preferences the student shares (Memory), so conversations feel personal.

Offline, LAFINA still handles **small talk**: greetings, "how are you", "who made you", "what can you do", thanks, and goodbyes. For general-knowledge questions, it tells the student to turn on Online Mode rather than guess.

## How the Two Work Together

Every message first goes through intent understanding:

| The message is… | LAFINA… |
|---|---|
| A planner change ("add a task…") | Prepares a **review card**. Nothing is saved until Confirm |
| A question about the student's plans ("what's on today?") | Answers from local data. Nothing is written |
| Conversation, trivia, or study help | Replies conversationally (DeepSeek online, local reply offline) |
| A USTP question | Answers with sources through Ask USTP |

A single message can mix them. For example, *"I'm a night owl, help me plan my week"* saves a memory **and** starts planning.

## Multi-Turn Interaction

LAFINA is multi-turn in several places:

* **Chat:** follow-ups like "move it to 4 PM" or "book the first one" refer to earlier messages. The review card can be edited, confirmed, or cancelled by voice or text ("yes", "make it 5 instead").
* **Reminder calls:** LAFINA starts the call, speaks the reminder, listens, interprets the reply ("give me ten minutes"), and closes the loop.
* **Call LAFINA:** a hands-free voice conversation, online (ElevenLabs + DeepSeek) or offline (Whisper + phone TTS).

## Intent Evaluation During Calls

During a reminder call, LAFINA must respond quickly. It uses an on-device keyword matcher that understands natural replies such as "okay", "got it", "five more minutes", or "in half an hour" and turns them into **acknowledge** or **snooze for N minutes**. It does not require exact words like "Yes" or "Stop".

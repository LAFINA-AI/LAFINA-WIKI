The LAFINA interaction flow is completely automated and runs in the background.

1. **Trigger**: The SQLite database hits a scheduled time for a task reminder.
2. **Call Initiation**: The React Native app triggers an outgoing phone call interface to immediately grab the user's attention.
3. **Delivery**: The Kokoro Text-to-Speech engine verbally delivers the pre-crafted message.
4. **Acknowledgment**: The user speaks to confirm receipt of the reminder. Whisper handles the Speech-to-Text, and SmolLM2 evaluates the intent.
5. **Termination**: The AI delivers a short closing statement and hangs up.

<CodeBlock title="Flow Example" language="text">
AI: "Hey, it's time for your Advanced Calculus study session. Are you ready to start?"
User: "Yeah, I'm opening the book now."
AI: "Great! Good luck with your session. Bye!"
[Call Ends]

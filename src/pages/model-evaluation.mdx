## Research Questions

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| **RQ1** | Does the phone's built-in TTS start speaking faster and use less memory than the earlier Kokoro engine, at acceptable clarity? |
| **RQ2** | How accurate and fast is on-device speech recognition (Whisper) for student academic commands without internet? |
| **RQ3** | Does the rule-based fast path shorten response time without causing more wrong acknowledgments or scheduling mistakes? |
| **RQ4** | How reliably are call-style reminders delivered, given permissions, app lifecycle, and phone-brand battery policies? |
| **RQ5** | How accurately does Ask USTP answer handbook questions, cite the right source, and decline unsupported questions? |

## What Is Measured

### Speech recognition (Whisper)
* **Word Error Rate (WER)** on a labeled set of student commands.
* **Latency** after the student stops talking. In-app targets: median about **1.5 s**, 95th percentile under **3 s**.
* Results are grouped by phone and room condition (quiet vs. noisy).

### Intent understanding
* **Accuracy and critical errors** on a labeled command set: clear commands, casual phrasing, Taglish, and relative times ("tomorrow", "next Friday").
* Compared across the fast-path parser only, each on-device model (SmolLM2, Gemma 3, Qwen 2.5), and DeepSeek.
* **Command completion time.** LAFINA has a built-in developer benchmark that records timing for each step: fast path, on-device model, and online extraction.

### Reminder delivery
* The share of alarms delivered within a set time window, in different states: app open, app closed, screen locked, after reboot.
* Results with permissions denied and with aggressive battery saving are recorded separately.

### Ask USTP (RAG)
* Retrieval recall, citation correctness, and correct "I couldn't verify that" answers on a gold question set with known source pages.

### Usability
* **System Usability Scale (SUS)** with student participants (informed consent), cross-checked against ISO/IEC 25010 quality characteristics: usability, performance efficiency, reliability, and security.

## Design Comparisons

<Card title="Rules vs. Language Models">
  Rules are instant and predictable but weak on casual phrasing. Models handle messy input but cost time, memory, and battery. LAFINA uses rules first and calls a model only when the rules aren't confident.
</Card>

<Card title="Small vs. Larger On-Device Models">
  SmolLM2-135M fits 3 GB phones. Qwen 2.5 0.5B and Gemma 3 1B handle Taglish and complex phrasing better but need more RAM. Students choose based on their phone.
</Card>

<Card title="On-Device vs. Cloud">
  On-device AI works without internet and keeps data private. Cloud AI (DeepSeek) is far better at open conversation and trivia. LAFINA uses both: on-device for the core, cloud as an optional extra.
</Card>

## Handling Imperfect English and Taglish

USTP students often switch between Filipino and English mid-sentence. The rule parser detects Taglish and colloquial phrasing and hands it to a model instead of trusting its own reading. Every interpretation is shown on a review card, so the student can correct it before anything is saved.

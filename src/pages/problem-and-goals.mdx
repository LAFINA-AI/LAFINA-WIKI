## Background & Statistical Context

The rapid evolution of digital technologies has shifted productivity tools from manual planners to AI assistants. The voice assistant market has reached massive scale:

Despite this scale, a significant gap remains: while people use voice assistants for calls, music, and searches, only **4.3%** use them for alarms or reminders — the most practical scheduling use case.

## The Localized Problem (Philippines Context)

The Philippines has high mobile penetration (over 90%) and roughly 57% of the population owns smartphones. Despite this, students face high cognitive loads balancing subjects, projects, and part-time work.

Relying on standard push notifications leads to **"alert fatigue"** — users become desensitized and ignore them. In contrast, healthcare studies show telephone call reminders can reduce missed appointments by up to:

<div style="display:flex; gap:1rem; flex-wrap:wrap; margin:1rem 0 1.5rem;">
  <StatCard value="39%" label="reduction in missed appointments with call reminders vs. push notifications" source="Mehranbod et al." color="warning" />
</div>

LAFINA applies this proven telephony advantage from healthcare directly to academic scheduling at USTP.

## General Objective

To design, develop, and evaluate **LAFINA**, a mobile-based AI Offline Scheduler application for the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP) in Cagayan de Oro (CDO), targeting students who struggle with academic deadlines.

## Specific Objectives

1. **Design** a voice-first mobile AI reminder application utilizing simulated phone calls.
2. **Implement** on-device natural language processing to convert voice into structured data (date, time, task) without internet connectivity.
3. **Test** system usability using the System Usability Scale (SUS).

## Scope and Limitations

<Card title="System Boundaries">
  The system strictly handles academic scheduling. It does **NOT** integrate with learning management systems or university portals.
  
  * It does not extract deadlines from social media or emails.
  * It is not a general knowledge chatbot.
  * It does not support iOS platforms or older 32-bit Android devices.
</Card>

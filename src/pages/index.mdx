## What is LAFINA?

**LAFINA** is an **AI student companion** for Android. It brings a student's classes, tasks, notes, reminders, and alarms into one app, and it is someone to talk to. Students can:

- **Plan** by tapping, typing, or speaking ("remind me to submit my lab report tomorrow at 5 PM").
- **Chat** about anything: small talk, general and trivia questions, explanations, and study help.
- **Study** with built-in skills: Pomodoro focus sessions, flashcards, quizzes, a GWA calculator, a summarizer, and lecture recordings.
- **Ask USTP** about handbook rules, academic dates, offices, and campus services, with sources shown.
- **Get called.** When something is due, LAFINA shows a simulated incoming call that the student answers and responds to by voice, instead of a banner that is easy to swipe away.

The planner works **offline**. Online Mode adds open conversation (DeepSeek), a natural live voice (ElevenLabs), and cloud sync.

Voice assistants are everywhere, but few people use them to stay on schedule, and push notifications are easy to ignore. LAFINA combines a companion people want to talk to with reminders that are hard to ignore.

## The LAFINA Approach

<Card title="A Companion, Not Just a Scheduler">
  With Online Mode, LAFINA holds a natural conversation, answers general questions, explains lessons, and remembers your preferences (for example, "I'm a night owl"). Offline, it still handles small talk and every supported planner command.
</Card>

<Card title="Offline-First Planning">
  Your plans live in a database on your phone. Tasks, notes, the calendar, alarms, and reminder calls work without internet. With an optional account, they sync when you are back online.
</Card>

<Card title="Proactive Reminder Calls">
  Instead of a silent banner, LAFINA rings with a full-screen call, even over the lock screen. Say "okay" to acknowledge or "give me ten minutes" to snooze, or use the buttons.
</Card>

<Card title="You Stay in Control">
  The AI proposes; you confirm. Any change to your planner appears as a review card first, and nothing is saved until you confirm it.
</Card>

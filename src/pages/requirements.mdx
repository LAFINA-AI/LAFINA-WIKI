To run LAFINA efficiently, specific hardware and software constraints must be met due to its offline-first AI operations.

### Hardware Requirements

- **OS**: Android 11.0+ (API 30)
- **Architecture**: 64-bit ARM64-v8a
- **Processor**: Octa-Core processor (2.0 GHz+)
- **Memory**: 4GB RAM minimum
- **Storage**: At least 1GB of free internal storage for the quantized AI models.

<Alert type="info" title="Storage Note">
  The 1GB storage requirement is necessary to securely hold the GGML binaries for Whisper, SmolLM2, and Kokoro entirely on-device.
</Alert>

### Software Stack

- **Framework**: React Native with TypeScript/JavaScript
- **Native Modules**: C++
- **Database**: SQLite
- **AI Backend**: GGML / llama.cpp

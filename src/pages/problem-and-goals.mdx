## Background & Statistical Context

The rapid evolution of digital technologies has shifted productivity tools from manual planners to AI assistants. Industry reports estimate over **8 billion** voice assistant devices are in use worldwide. Voice search adoption is expanding, with **20 to 25 percent** of internet users relying on voice queries regularly. 

Empirical studies show AI assistants can improve productivity by up to 40 percent depending on task complexity (Noy & Zhang, 2023). However, a significant gap remains: while people use voice for calls or messages, only **4.3 percent** use them for alarms or reminders.

## The Localized Problem (Philippines Context)

The Philippines has high mobile penetration (over 90 percent), and roughly 57 percent of the population owns smartphones. Despite this, students face high cognitive loads balancing subjects, projects, and part-time work. 

Relying on standard push notifications leads to **"alert fatigue"**, where users become desensitized and ignore them. In contrast, healthcare studies show telephone call reminders can reduce missed appointments by up to **39 percent**.

## General Objective

To design, develop, and evaluate **LAFINA**, a mobile-based AI Offline Scheduler application for the University of Science and Technology of Southern Philippines (USTP) in Cagayan de Oro (CDO), targeting students who struggle with academic deadlines.

## Specific Objectives

1. **Design** a voice-first mobile AI reminder application utilizing simulated phone calls.
2. **Implement** on-device natural language processing to convert voice into structured data (date, time, task) without internet connectivity.
3. **Test** system usability using the System Usability Scale (SUS).

## Scope and Limitations

<Card title="System Boundaries">
  The system strictly handles academic scheduling. It does **NOT** integrate with learning management systems or university portals.
  
  * It does not extract deadlines from social media or emails.
  * It is not a general knowledge chatbot.
  * It does not support iOS platforms or older 32-bit Android devices.
</Card>

## What is LAFINA?

**LAFINA** is a mobile-based AI application designed to combat **notification fatigue** among university students. Instead of passive push notifications that are easily ignored, LAFINA takes a proactive, voice-first approach — it **initiates simulated phone calls** to ensure tasks are actually acknowledged and schedules are actually followed.

Every component of LAFINA runs entirely on the user's device. No cloud. No internet required. No voice data transmitted anywhere.

## The LAFINA Approach

<Card title="Offline-First Architecture">
  LAFINA runs entirely on-device. Whisper.cpp (STT), SmolLM2-135M (NLU), and Kokoro 82M (TTS) all execute via GGML binaries on the phone's local CPU — no Wi-Fi, no cloud, no data charges required.
</Card>

<Card title="Proactive Voice Calls">
  Instead of a silent push notification banner, LAFINA triggers the device's native ringing mechanism — the same one used for actual phone calls. Humans are conditioned to answer calls. Notifications, not so much.
</Card>

<Card title="Closed-Loop Confirmation">
  The AI doesn't just deliver the reminder — it verifies acknowledgment. If you reply "give me five more minutes," SmolLM2 understands and re-queues the reminder. The loop closes only when you've truly acknowledged.
</Card>
